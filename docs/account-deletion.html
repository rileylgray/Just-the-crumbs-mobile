Delete your Just The Crumbs account

You can permanently delete your Just The Crumbs account and associated data at any time. Here's how.

Delete from within the app

  1. Open Just The Crumbs.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm in the dialog. If you signed in with Google, you may be asked to sign in again to confirm.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Prefer to request it by email?

If you can't access the app, email realgamesrealfun@outlook.com from the address associated with your account (or include your account details), and we will delete your account and data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email, profile photo reference)
  • Your recipes and the comments stored on them
  • Your recipe categories
  • Your shared recipe links

What is kept, and for how long

  • Abuse reports you filed are retained for safety and moderation purposes.
  • Comments you left on other users' public recipes may remain visible.
  • Residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days.
